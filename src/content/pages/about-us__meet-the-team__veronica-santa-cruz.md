###### PA-C, Medical Aesthetic Specialist

### Veronica Santa Cruz

Veronica Santa Cruz is native to the Monterey Peninsula area. She attended Hartnell Community College and graduated from the nursing program in 2012.

After receiving her nursing degree, Veronica moved to southern California to pursue a career in cosmetics, she got her start in the cosmetic industry working for Dr. Laser in Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, and Manhattan Beach. After moving back to Monterey to be near her parents, Veronica worked for over 3 years doing cosmetic dermatology in Monterey. Veronica has completed extensive training at the Allergan Medical Institute.

Aside from practicing aesthetics she enjoys gardening and spending time with her friends and family. We are very fortunate to have Veronica join us as part of the Salinas Valley Medical Aesthetics family. Veronica’s artistic eye and extensive knowledge of anti-aging treatments make her a huge asset to our practice.

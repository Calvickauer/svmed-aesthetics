###### Registered Nurse located in Salinas, CA

### Michael Daniels

Originally from New Jersey, Michael gained experience in cosmetics and mastery of laser treatments working at a busy clinic in New York.

He first came to California as a traveling nurse working in surgery in 2013. He fell in love with the west coast and since 2017 has lived in Monterey. Michael continues to work in surgery at Salinas Valley Memorial Hospital. He began working at Salinas Valley Medical Aesthetics in June 2021 as our laser specialist and has been formally trained in our neurotoxin products and fillers.

He is a fantastic baker and enjoys wine tasting with friends. Michael’s easy-going demeanor and attention to the small details make him a wonderful provider and we are proud to have him as part of the Salinas Valley Medical Aesthetics family!

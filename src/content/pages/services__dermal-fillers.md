### Dermal Fillers

###### Restore a youthful appearance and smooth lines with dermal fillers

Dermal fillers are an easy and convenient way to enhance natural beauty and restore a youthful appearance. They address age-related issues, such as fat loss or migration, collagen loss, bone resorption, and fine lines and wrinkles. Hyaluronic acid or calcium-based fillers add volume and hydration and contour the face, providing immediate results. Here at Salinas Valley Medical Aesthetics, we carry a variety of HA fillers, including Juvéderm and Restylane brand fillers, as well as Radiesse. Talk to your provider to see which product is right for you. Add [neuromodulation](/services/neuromodulators/) to your treatment for total rejuvenation and a beautiful outcome!

Because every patient is unique, we recommend an in-person consultation with one of our injectors to evaluate and create a plan. Fillers vary by their consistency, longevity, substance, density, and more. Your provider will select the right filler for your skin type, age, bone structure, and your unique concerns and goals during your personalized, one-on-one consultation.

**Q:** **What are dermal fillers?**

**A:** Dermal fillers are injectable substances used to add volume and fullness to areas of the face, such as the cheeks, lips, chin and under the eyes. They can help reduce the appearance of wrinkles, fine lines, and sagging skin, providing a more youthful appearance. Most fillers are made from hyaluronic acid (HA), a smooth gel-like substance, which is naturally present in your skin. Fillers add structure and support to your skin leaving you with natural rejuvenated results.

**Q:** **Who is a good candidate for dermal fillers?**

**A:** Fillers are great for anyone who wants to restore lost volume or add volume to areas like lips, cheeks, or the under-eye area, as well as individuals seeking to reduce the appearance of aging signs. It’s important for potential candidates to consult with a qualified practitioner to assess their specific needs and determine if dermal fillers are appropriate for them.

**Q: How long do fillers last?**

**A:** Results typically last several months to a couple of years. The longevity of dermal fillers varies depending on the type used and the treatment area. **Hyaluronic Acid Fillers** typically last 6 to 12 months, depending on the specific product and area treated. Calcium-based fillers usually last 9-15 months. Factors like individual metabolism, the treatment area, and lifestyle choices can also influence how long the effects last. Regular treatments to restore volume can help maintain the desired look.

**Q: Is there an age restriction to get filler?**

**A:** We recommend filler patients be at least 21, as the skin and underlying tissue is still developing in younger individuals. We will provide recommendations for natural looking results.

**Q: What to expect after treatment:**

**A:** Immediately following your treatment, you may experience some tenderness in the area. Bruising is usually minimal, but may last up to two weeks. Swelling is common and usually resolves within two weeks. The product is placed in specific areas, so please avoid facials, massages, saunas or excessive pressure for at least 3 days.

* Smile lines/marionette lines
* Cheek enhancement
* Nose augmentation
* Chin/jawline
* Under eyes
* Temples
* Hands

###### Office Manager & CoolSculpting Specialist

### Welcome Luz Ruelas to SVMA!!

**Office Manager and Certified CoolSculpting Technician, Luz has been an essential part of Salinas Valley Medical Aesthetics since 2012.**

She is a graduate of multiple different CoolSculpting University trainings, and has performed thousands of treatments over the last ten years. She is a vital asset to our team and is deeply valued and appreciated by our patients. Incredibly caring and easy to talk to, Luz conducts your consultation in a judgment-free and results-driven manner. Her number one goal is for you to be happy and feel good, and she will formulate a personalized treatment plan to achieve the best possible outcome.

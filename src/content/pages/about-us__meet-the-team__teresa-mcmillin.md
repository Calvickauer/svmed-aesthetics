###### PA-C, MPAS, Owner

### Teresa McMillin

Teresa McMillin, PA-C, MPAS, has worked as a cosmetic dermatology and family medicine specialist since 2004.

In her role at Salinas Valley Medical Aesthetics in Salinas, California, Teresa combines art and medicine to help her patients look and feel their very best. She always focuses on beautiful and natural results! Teresa offers Botox®, Juvéderm®, laser hair removal, laser skin rejuvenation, and much more.

Teresa chose to specialize in the area of cosmetic dermatology because she has a genuine passion for helping patients look and feel great. Teresa also completes 100 hours of continuing medical education every two years and takes a certification exam every six years to maintain board certification in the area of family practice. She maintains active memberships in the American Association of Physician Assistants, the California Association of Physician Assistants, and the Society of Dermatology Physician Assistants. Teresa is highly experienced in the area of injectables like Botox, and for many years she was an injection trainer and regular speaker for Medicis Aesthetics.

In her free time, Teresa enjoys spending quality time with her husband, son, and dogs. She also loves cycling and other fitness activities in the beautiful Salinas area.

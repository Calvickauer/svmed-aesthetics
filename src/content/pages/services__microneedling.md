### Microneedling

Experience the next level of skin rejuvenation with microneedling using the CosmoPen. This advanced, FDA-approved device is designed to deliver precise and controlled microneedling treatments, stimulating your skin’s natural healing process. By creating tiny micro-channels in the skin, microneedling stimulates collagen production, resulting in smoother texture, reduced fine lines and wrinkles, and minimized acne scars and appearance of pores. Whether you’re looking to refresh your complexion or address specific skin concerns, microneedling with the CosmoPen offers effective, long-lasting results with minimal downtime.

Also known as collagen induction therapy, microneedling stimulates your skin to produce its own natural collagen by creating small punctures over the surface of the skin (3 million micro-channels per treatment). As you age, collagen and elastin naturally decrease, causing fine lines and wrinkles. It is a safe and effective way to boost collagen production and leaves you with glowing and youthful looking skin. This highly effective treatment can not only reduce signs of aging but enhance your skin’s overall appearance, too.

**Q: Who is it for?**

**A:** Microneedling is safe for all skin types and promotes care-free healing to address acne scars, fine lines, and wrinkles. It’s a fast and safe method that initiates the production of collagen and elastin in your skin.

**Q: How does it work?**

**A:** The amazing thing about our bodies is that they have natural processes that react to injury by producing new cells and extracellular materials (like elastin and collagen) to heal. The CosmoPen initiates this natural process with a finely-tuned microneedling pen by creating hundreds of temporary micro-sized openings in your skin. These small openings are created with the CosmoPen’s disposable needle cartridge.

While multiple sessions are typically recommended to achieve optimal results depending on your specific goals, noticeable changes can be seen just one week after treatment and gradually improve as the body’s healing process begins to take effect. Side effects are similar to a mild sunburn, such as redness, tenderness, and some swelling. Dryness for a few days after is common.

**Q: What is Microneedling with PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)?**

**A:** PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin) is a cutting-edge, natural treatment that harnesses the healing power of your own blood containing proteins, stem cells, and growth factors to rejuvenate and revitalize your skin. By concentrating growth factors and platelets, PRF stimulates collagen production and promotes tissue repair for a youthful, refreshed appearance. The red blood cells are removed, and the PRF is infused into the micro-punctures, nourishing the cells and delivering a glowing complexion. Depending on your specific concern, your provider may also apply a brightening, hydrating, or an anti-aging serum.

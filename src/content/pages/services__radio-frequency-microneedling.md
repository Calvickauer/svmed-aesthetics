### Radiofrequency Microneedling

![](img:2023/06/logo.png)

###### Skin tightening & improve fine lines and scars with RF MN

The VirtueRF Microneedling Platform is the most advanced RF micro needling device available today. The combination of radio-frequency and micro needling is a proven way to stimulate collagen production to target fine lines, wrinkles, and skin laxity and improve skin tone and texture. It is also a great way to prevent skin laxity and collagen loss.

VirtueRF is the only expandable RF micro needling platform with three unique handpieces that treat from head to toe. Smart RF is used to treat the face, neck, décolleté, and hands. DeepRF treats larger body areas like the abdomen and arms. ExactRF targets delicate areas around the eyes, mouth, and jawline.

###### The Virtue RF can treat:

* Fine lines and wrinkles
* Under-eye bags
* Skin laxity- on almost any area of the body
* Scarring and stretch marks

The procedure is very comfortable and there is no down time. You may feel mild heat during the treatment, and you will experience some redness afterwards, which may last a few hours. We will apply a soothing mask to help with recovery, or give you some after care products, depending on the area being treated.

For the best results, we recommend three treatments, one month apart. Please arrive to your appointment with a clean face with no makeup.

Ask your provider what the Virtue RF can do for you!

**What’s the difference between Morpheus8 and Virtue RF?**

Both of these popular platforms are the same type of technology using radiofrequency and microneedling  for tissue remodeling and to stimulate collagen production. The Virtue RF, however, offers a more customizable approach to treatments. With this platform, we can provide a more targeted and controlled delivery of the RF energy.

* Customizable- We can adjust the exact amount of energy being delivered into the skin to treat a wider range of skin concerns. Three different hand pieces allow us to safely and effectively treat anywhere on the body, including, for example, the face, jowls, abdomen, and the delicate skin under the eyes.
* Comfort- The insulated needles and the robotic arm needle delivery system ensures a more comfortable treatment experience, as opposed to the “staple-gun” delivery system of the Morpheus8.
* Side effects- The Morpheus8 has a higher incidence of adverse events, such as burns, scarring, and hyperpigmentation.
